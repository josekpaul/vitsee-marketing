About VitSee

Healthcare pricing shouldn't be a secret.

We built VitSee to synthesize massive, fragmented healthcare pricing data into insights a real person can use — before the bill arrives, not after.

TBs of pricing data processed
1000s of ACA plans
100,000s of providers
Millions of negotiated rates

The problem

Under federal transparency rules, insurers and hospitals now publish their negotiated rates — but as terabytes of sprawling, inconsistent machine-readable files that no consumer could ever read. The result: people still choose care blind, often paying thousands more than they needed to for the exact same procedure a few miles away.

What we do

VitSee ingests ACA Public Use Files, CMS Transparency in Coverage machine-readable files, and CMS quality data, then turns that firehose into something simple: a clear comparison of what a procedure will actually cost you, at which providers, ranked by both price and quality.

Who it's for

Anyone shopping for an ACA health plan, or anyone who wants to know what a procedure will cost before they book it. VitSee exists so that the choice between "cheap" and "good" is never one you have to guess at.

What we believe

Our approach

Transparency by default

If the data exists, you should be able to see it — in plain language, not a 2TB JSON file.

Price and quality, together

The cheapest option only matters if it's also good care. We never rank one without the other.

Built for the real decision

Estimates reflect your actual plan — deductible, coinsurance, and all — not a generic sticker price.
