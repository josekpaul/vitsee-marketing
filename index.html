Why now

Healthcare costs keep climbing. The tools to shop for it haven't kept up.

Premiums keep rising and government subsidies keep shrinking, so more of every bill lands on you. For shoppable care — the scans, tests, and procedures you can plan ahead for — price and quality should be visible before you book, not buried in a bill afterward.

Up to 13x price variation

In one Ohio network we analyzed, the same procedure on the same insurer ranged 8–13x in price. Read the full analysis →

Federal rules, real teeth

Federal rules already require insurers to publish negotiated rates. States are starting to add enforcement — Ohio's HB 173, for example, gives patients a legal right to see exact prices before they receive care.
